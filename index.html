🫧 GaussianPerl

Turn one photo into a 3D scene you can walk around — right on this device.

Local AI models estimate depth, and as you move the camera they paint in whatever the photo never saw — so you keep getting a picture that looks like it was taken from there.

Photos never leave your device. The depth (25–75 MB, depending on your device) and fill (~28 MB) models download once and are cached.
